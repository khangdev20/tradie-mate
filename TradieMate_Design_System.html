Design Rules v2.0
NEVER
✕ Never use dark, blurry, or colored decorative drop shadows: Only hairline borders and soft micro-elevation are permitted for surface separation (See Cards example).
✕ Never use multi-color gradients for interface backgrounds: Only subtle single-tone directional sheens are permitted on interactive gesture sliders (See Slide to Confirm).
✕ Never use font weight 700 (Bold): Maximum weight is 600 (Semi-bold) for display headings and micro-badges (See Typography Scale).
✕ Never create touch targets smaller than 44px: Strictly enforced hit-areas to support one-handed use and work gloves (See Buttons & Chips).
✕ Never use emojis in place of system icons: Use standardized vector outline icons from MaterialCommunityIcons (See Icons Grid).
ALWAYS (WITH LIVE EXAMPLES)
✓ Always use warm canvas #FAFAF8: Maximizes glare resistance and readability under harsh sunlight (See Background Swatch).
✓ Always show unified offline & sync status: Informs the technician and queues actions when out of cell reception (See Unified Network Bar).
✓ Always make 'Next Job' the largest card on Dashboard: Serves as the immediate operational focal point (See Next Job Card).
✓ Always itemize 10% GST on all invoices: Legally required by Australian Tax Office (ATO) regulations (See Australian GST Engine).
✓ Always design for the natural Thumb Zone: Pin primary action buttons and navigation to the bottom half of the screen (See Bottom Navigation).
